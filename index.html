Questions

Common questions before a project starts

What does Logic Scale Tech do?

We design and build mobile apps, websites and no-code tools, and we run SEO and digital marketing so they get found. We are based in Mumbai and work with clients across India.

Do you build both Android and iOS apps?

Yes. We build with React Native and Expo, so one codebase can ship to Google Play and the App Store.

Can you do SEO for a website you did not build?

Yes. We start with a technical and content audit of your current site, fix what holds back your rankings, and track the enquiries it brings in.

What is a no-code app and when does it make sense?

A no-code app is built with visual tools instead of custom code. It suits internal tools, trackers and early versions of a product that need to go live quickly and at a lower cost.
