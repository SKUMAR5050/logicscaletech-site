Contact QR code generator

Make a QR code for your contact details. Add your name, phone number, email, company and website. Anyone who scans it can save you as a contact in one tap.

Private by design: your details are processed on your device and are never sent to our servers.

What is a contact QR code?

A contact QR code, also called a vCard QR code, holds your contact card inside a QR code. When someone points their phone camera at it, the phone shows your name, number and email and offers to save you as a new contact. Nobody has to type your number, and nothing is spelled wrongly.

This free generator makes the code in your browser. You can download it as a PNG picture, as an SVG for sharp printing, or save the same details as a .vcf contact file.

How to make a QR code for your contact details

  1. Type your full name and phone number. Include the country code, such as +91.
  2. Add the details you want: email, company, job title, website or address. Leave the rest empty.
  3. Check the code on screen. It updates as you type.
  4. Choose a colour and a PNG size. Use 1024 px or more for printing.
  5. Download the PNG or SVG. Scan it once with your own phone before you share or print it.

What can you put in a contact QR code?

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Company name
  • Job title
  • Website
  • Address or city

The more you add, the denser the code becomes and the larger you should print it. For a small visiting card, name, phone and email are enough.

How to scan a contact QR code

  • iPhone: open the Camera app and point it at the code. Tap the banner, then Add Contact.
  • Android: open the Camera app or Google Lens and point it at the code. Tap the suggestion, then Add contact.
  • No scanner on the phone? Use a QR scanner app. The ScanOrbit app reads QR codes and barcodes and works offline.

Where to use a contact QR code

  • On a visiting card, so people save you without typing.
  • On a shop board, clinic desk, gym or salon counter, so customers can save your number.
  • On a poster, flyer or banner for an event.
  • In an email signature, a PDF brochure or a presentation.
  • On a name badge or ID card at a conference.

Tips for a QR code that scans every time

  • Print it at least 2 cm wide with a white border around it.
  • Use a dark colour on a white background. Black, navy and dark green work best.
  • Keep the details short. Fewer details make a simpler code that scans faster.
  • If you print it small or on rough paper, choose Strong (Q) or Strongest (H) under Error correction.
  • Always test with your own phone before you print a hundred cards.

Questions

How do I make a QR code for my contact details?

Type your name and phone number in the boxes above. Add an email, company, job title, website or address if you want. The QR code appears as you type. Download it as PNG or SVG and print it or share it.

What happens when someone scans a contact QR code?

Their phone camera reads the code and offers to save a new contact with your name, number, email and the other details you added. They tap Add Contact, and you are saved in their phone.

How do I scan a contact QR code?

Open the normal Camera app on your phone and point it at the code. On iPhone and most Android phones a banner appears. Tap it, then choose Add Contact. If your Android camera does not read QR codes, open Google Lens or use a QR scanner app such as ScanOrbit.

Is this contact QR code generator free?

Yes. It is free, with no watermark, no sign-up and no limit on how many codes you make, including for business use.

Is it a vCard QR code?

Yes. The code holds your details in the vCard format, which is the standard contact card format that iPhone and Android phones understand. You can also download the same details as a .vcf contact file.

Do you keep my details?

No. The code is made in your browser, so your name, number and email never reach our server.

Will the code stop working after some time?

No. Your details are stored inside the code itself, so it never expires and does not depend on this website. If your phone number changes, make a new code.

Will it work on any phone and with any QR scanner?

Yes. It is a normal QR code, so any modern phone camera or QR scanner app can read it. Include the country code, such as +91, in the phone number so it also works for people in other countries.

What is the best size to print a contact QR code?

Print it at least 2 cm wide for a visiting card, and larger for a poster or shop board. Keep a white border around it, use a dark colour on white, and scan it once with your own phone before you print.
