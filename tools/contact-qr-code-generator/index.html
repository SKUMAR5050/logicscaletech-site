Contact QR code generator

Add a name, a phone number and an email. Anyone who scans the code can save you as a contact.

Private by design: your files are processed on your device and are never sent to our servers.

Put a contact QR code on a visiting card, a poster or a shop board. A phone camera reads it and offers to add the contact, so nobody has to type your number.

The ScanOrbit app makes the same Contact QR codes.

How to use

  1. Type your name and phone number. Add an email if you like.
  2. Check the details, since they go into the code exactly as typed.
  3. Download the code and print it on your card or poster.

Tips

  • Include the country code in the phone number, such as +91, so it works from anywhere.
  • Keep it short. The fewer details, the simpler and easier to scan the code.
  • Scan it once with your own phone before you print.

Questions

How do I make a QR code for my contact details?

Choose Contact, type your name, phone and optional email, and download the code.

What happens when someone scans it?

Their phone offers to save a new contact with your name, number and email.

Is it free?

Yes, with no watermark and no sign-up.

Do you keep my details?

No. The code is made in your browser, and the details never reach a server.
