    SSC online forms need a photo and a signature as small JPG files. This tool gives you both in the sizes built into the ScanOrbit app, and you can type another size if your notice says something different.

    Sizes here are the ones built into the ScanOrbit app. Rules can change, so check your form notice before you upload.

    How to use

    1. Pick SSC exam photo or SSC signature from the list.
    2. Add your picture and press Make picture.
    3. Download the JPG and upload it on the form.

    Tips

    • Use a recent photo with a plain light background and your face clearly visible.
    • Use Crop to fill for the photo and Fit inside for the signature.
    • Open the downloaded file once to check it before you upload.

    Questions

    What is the SSC photo size in this tool?

    The SSC photo is set to 3.5 x 4.5 cm and 20-50 KB. Always check the current notice for your exam.

    What is the SSC signature size in this tool?

    The SSC signature is set to 6 x 2 cm and 10-20 KB. Always check the current notice.

    Is my photo uploaded?

    No. The picture is resized in your browser and never leaves your device.

    What if my exam needs a different size?

    Choose My own size and type the pixels and KB from your notice.
