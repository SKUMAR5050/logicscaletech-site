UPI QR code generator

Enter your UPI ID, add a name or an amount if you like, and get a QR code that any UPI app can scan.

Private by design: your files are processed on your device and are never sent to our servers.

Customers scan the code with any UPI app and the payment details are filled in for them. Add an amount if the price is fixed, or leave it empty so the payer types it.

The ScanOrbit app has the same UPI payment QR maker, which works offline.

How to use

  1. Type your UPI ID, for example name@bank.
  2. Add your name, and an amount if you want one.
  3. Download the code, then test it with a small payment before you print it.

Tips

  • Always test with a small payment first, and confirm it reaches the right account.
  • Your UPI ID is the only thing that decides where the money goes, so check every letter.
  • Leave the amount empty if prices change from sale to sale.

Questions

How do I make a UPI QR code?

Choose UPI payment, enter your UPI ID, add a name and optional amount, and download the code.

Can I add a fixed amount?

Yes. Fill in the amount and the payer's app will show it. Leave it empty to let them type any amount.

Is this an official bank QR code?

No. It is a standard UPI payment link in QR form, made by this tool. Check it with a small test payment before using it for a business.

Do you store my UPI ID?

No. The code is made in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
