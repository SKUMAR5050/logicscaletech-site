Free QR code generator

Make a QR code for WhatsApp, UPI payment, Wi-Fi, a website link, a contact and more. Choose what it is for, fill in the details, and download it as PNG or SVG.

Private by design: your files are processed on your device and are never sent to our servers.

Pick the type: WhatsApp chat, UPI payment, Wi-Fi, website link, contact, email, SMS, phone call or plain text. The QR code appears as you type, and nothing is sent to a server.

You can choose a colour, the error correction level and the PNG size. The codes work with any phone camera or QR scanner app.

How to use

  1. Choose the type, such as WhatsApp chat, UPI payment, Wi-Fi or Website link.
  2. Fill in the details. The QR code updates as you type.
  3. Pick a colour and size, then download it as PNG to share or SVG to print.

Tips

  • Print the QR code at least 2 cm wide, with a white border around it, and scan it once before you share it.
  • Dark code on a white background scans best.
  • For a WhatsApp QR code, a 10-digit Indian number works, and the country code 91 is added for you.
  • If you print the code small, choose Strong (Q) or Strongest (H) under Error correction.
  • These codes hold your details directly, so they never expire.

Questions

How do I make a QR code?

Choose the type, fill in the details, and download the picture. It takes a few seconds.

What kinds of QR code can I make?

You can make a QR code for a WhatsApp chat, a UPI payment, Wi-Fi, a website link, a contact card, an email, an SMS, a phone call, or any text.

How do I make a WhatsApp QR code?

Choose WhatsApp chat, type the number (a 10-digit Indian number is fine) and an optional ready message. Anyone who scans the code opens a WhatsApp chat with you.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. The details are stored inside the code itself, so it never expires and does not depend on this website.

Is it free for business use?

Yes. There is no watermark and no limit.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The QR code is drawn in your browser, so the details you type never reach a server.

Which colour and size should I choose?

Dark colours on white scan best. Use 1024 px or larger for printing. If the code will be printed small, choose a stronger error correction.
