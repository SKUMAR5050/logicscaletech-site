How to use

  1. Choose the type: Wi-Fi, UPI payment, Contact or Message or link.

Tips

  • Print the QR code at least 2 cm wide, with a white border around it, and scan it once before you share it.
  • Dark code on a white background scans best.
  • These codes hold your details directly, so they never expire.
