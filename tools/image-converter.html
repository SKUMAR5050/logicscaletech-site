    Some websites and forms only take JPG, some tools only work with PNG, and WebP files are often not accepted by older software. This tool changes the format in your browser.

    PNG files keep sharp edges and transparency, but they are larger. JPG files are smaller, which helps when a form has a KB limit. When you convert to JPG, transparent areas turn white.

    How to use

    1. Choose the format you want: JPG, PNG or WebP.
    2. Add your pictures.
    3. Press Convert, then tap Download next to each file.

    Tips

    • For a smaller file, convert to JPG and lower the quality a little.
    • Use PNG for logos and screenshots.
    • To reach an exact size in KB, use the Compress to KB tool after converting.

    Questions

    How do I convert PNG to JPG?

    Choose JPG, add your PNG file, press Convert and download the result.

    Does the quality drop?

    JPG and WebP are lossy. At 90 the difference is usually hard to see, and you can raise it to 100. PNG does not lose quality.

    Can I convert many files at once?

    Yes. Add several pictures and each one is converted.

    Are my pictures uploaded?

    No. They are converted in your browser and never leave your device.
