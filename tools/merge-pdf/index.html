Merge PDF files into one

Add your PDFs, arrange them in order, and download one combined file. Nothing is uploaded.

Drop PDFs here or tap to chooseSelect two or more PDF files.

    Private by design: your files are processed on your device and are never sent to our servers.

    Joining a form, an ID proof and a certificate into one PDF is a common job. Add your files here, set the order, and merge them.

    In the ScanOrbit app the same job lives in Merge & split PDF, and it also works offline.

    How to use

    1. Add two or more PDF files.
    2. Use the arrows to set the order.
    3. Press Merge PDFs and download the result.

    Tips

    • Check the order before you merge. It is easy to move a file up or down with the arrows.
    • Password-protected PDFs cannot be merged. Remove the password first.
    • Need only some pages? Use Split PDF first, then merge.

    Questions

    How do I combine PDF files into one?

    Add the PDFs, put them in order with the arrows, and press Merge PDFs.

    Is there a limit on size or pages?

    There is no set limit. Very large files depend on your device memory.

    Are my PDFs uploaded?

    No. Merging happens in your browser and the files stay on your device.

    Why did one of my PDFs fail to open?

    Password-protected PDFs cannot be merged. Remove the password and try again.
