Split a PDF or extract pages

Choose a PDF, enter the pages you want, and download the result. Nothing is uploaded.

Drop a PDF here or tap to chooseOne PDF file.

    Private by design: your files are processed on your device and are never sent to our servers.

    Sometimes you only need a few pages of a long PDF, such as the page with a signature or an ID. Enter the page numbers and get a new, smaller PDF.

    In the ScanOrbit app this is part of Merge & split PDF, so the same job works on your phone.

    How to use

    1. Choose your PDF. The page count appears.
    2. Type the pages you want, such as 1-3, 5, 8-10.
    3. Pick one combined PDF or separate files, then press Split PDF.

    Tips

    • Page numbers start at 1, the first page of the file.
    • Pick separate PDFs if you want every range as its own file.
    • Want to put pages back together? Use Merge PDF.

    Questions

    How do I extract pages from a PDF?

    Choose the PDF, type the pages or ranges such as 2-4, 7, keep the output on One PDF, and press Split PDF.

    Can I split a PDF into several files?

    Yes. Choose A separate PDF for each part, and every comma-separated range becomes its own file.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. The PDF is processed inside your browser and never leaves your device.

    Does it work on a phone?

    Yes, on modern phone browsers. Very large PDFs are easier on a computer.
