    How to use

    1. Pick the country and document from the list.
    2. Add your photo and keep Crop to fill.
    3. Press Make photo and download the JPG.

    Tips

    • Use a recent photo, facing the camera, with a plain light background and no shadows.
    • Crop to fill centres the picture, so start with a photo where your face is in the middle.

    Questions

    What is the passport photo size for the USA?

    In this tool the USA passport and visa photo is 2 × 2 inches, 600 × 600 pixels, up to 240 KB.

    Which countries are included?

    USA, India (abroad), UK, Schengen, Canada, Australia, UAE, China and Japan, plus a standard 35 × 45 mm size.

    Is my photo uploaded?

    No. It is cut and resized in your browser and never leaves your device.

    Does it check if my photo is accepted?

    No. It only sets the size. Each country has other rules on pose, background and lighting, so read the official guide.
