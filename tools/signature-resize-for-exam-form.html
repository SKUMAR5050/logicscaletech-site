    Many online forms ask for a signature as a small JPG, often between 10 and 20 KB. A phone photo of a signature is far bigger than that, so the form rejects it.

    This tool resizes and compresses the signature in your browser. Pick an exam size from the list, or choose My own size and type the pixels and KB yourself. Sizes here are the ones built into the ScanOrbit app. Rules can change, so check your form notice before you upload.

    How to use

    1. Sign in black or blue ink on plain white paper and take a clear photo in good light.
    2. Pick the size from the list, or choose My own size.
    3. Add your picture, press Make picture, and download the JPG.

    Tips

    • Use Fit inside for signatures, so nothing gets cut off.
    • Keep the paper flat and the light even, with no shadows across the signature.
    • If the file is smaller than the minimum, retake the photo with a thicker pen or closer.

    Questions

    What size should my signature be?

    It depends on the form. In this tool the SSC signature is 6 x 2 cm and 10-20 KB, and the bank exam signature is 140 x 60 px and 10-20 KB. Always check the current notice.

    Can I type my own size?

    Yes. Choose My own size, then type the width and height in pixels and the largest size in KB.

    Is my signature uploaded anywhere?

    No. It is resized in your browser and never leaves your device.

    Why does the form still reject my file?

    Check the file type (JPG), the pixels and the KB limit in the notice. Some forms also need a minimum size.
