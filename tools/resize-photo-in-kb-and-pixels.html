    Online forms for exams and jobs ask for a photo in a fixed size, such as 200 x 230 pixels and 20 to 50 KB. This tool lets you set the size yourself: pixels, cm, mm or inches, and the smallest and largest file size in KB.

    It can reduce a photo to 20 KB, 50 KB or 100 KB, and it can also make a small file larger when the form asks for a minimum size. Pick one of the ready sizes for SSC, bank exams, UPSC or NEET, or type your own. Rules change, so check your form notice before you upload.

    How to use

    1. Choose My own size and type the width, height and KB from your notice. Or pick a ready size from the list.
    2. Add your photo or signature and press Make picture.
    3. Check the size shown, then download the JPG and upload it to your form.

    Tips

    • Use the smallest KB box when the form says minimum size, and the largest KB box when it says maximum.
    • Leave width and height empty to change only the KB and keep the picture’s own pixels.
    • For a signature, sign on plain white paper with a dark pen and choose Fit inside.
    • If the file is still too big, try a smaller width and height.

    Questions

    How do I reduce a photo to 50 KB?

    Choose My own size, type 50 in Largest file size (KB), add your photo and press Make picture. You can also type the width and height in pixels.

    How do I increase a photo to 20 KB or more?

    Type 20 in Smallest file size (KB). If your photo is smaller than that, the tool makes the file larger by adding invisible padding, so the picture looks the same.

    Can I set the size in pixels, cm or inches?

    Yes. Choose the unit, then type width and height. For cm, mm or inches the tool uses the DPI box, and 300 DPI is a good default.

    Which sizes do exams ask for?

    Each exam has its own size and KB limit, and it can change every year. Copy the numbers from your exam notice into the boxes, or pick a ready size for SSC, bank exams, UPSC or NEET.

    Does it work for signature and thumb print?

    Yes. Use the exact width, height and KB from the form. Choose Fit inside if you do not want the picture cropped.

    Is my photo uploaded?

    No. Everything happens in your browser and your photo never leaves your phone or computer.

    Why can the tool not reach my KB size?

    A very large or very detailed photo may not fit a small KB limit at a big pixel size. Use a smaller pixel size, or leave width and height empty so the tool can shrink it for you.
