GST calculator: add or remove GST

Enter an amount and a GST rate. See the price before GST, the GST amount and the final price.

Enter the GST rate that applies to your item or service. GST rates can change, so check the current rate on the official GST website or with your accountant.

Use Add GST when you have the price before tax and want the final price. Use Remove GST when the price already includes GST and you want to find the price before tax and the tax amount.

The result also shows the split between CGST and SGST, which is half each for a sale inside one state. For a sale between states, the full amount is IGST.

How to use

  1. Choose Add GST or Remove GST.
  2. Type the amount and the GST rate.
  3. Read the price before GST, the GST amount and the final price.

Tips

  • Round the final figures the way your invoice software does, to avoid small differences.
  • For an inter-state sale, show the full GST amount as IGST, not as CGST and SGST.
  • This tool is a helper and not tax advice. Ask your accountant if you are unsure.

Questions

How do I add GST to a price?

Multiply the price by the rate and divide by 100 to get the GST, then add it to the price. For 18 percent on 1000, the GST is 180 and the total is 1180.

How do I remove GST from a price?

Divide the price that includes GST by (1 + rate/100). For 1180 at 18 percent, the price before GST is 1000.

What are CGST and SGST?

For a sale inside one state, GST is split equally into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). Between states, it is charged as IGST.

Which GST rate should I use?

Use the rate that applies to your item or service. Rates can change, so check the official GST website or ask your accountant.
