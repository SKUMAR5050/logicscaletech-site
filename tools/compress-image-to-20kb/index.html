Compress image to 20 KB

Drop your photo, keep 20 as the target, and get a file that fits. It all happens in your browser.

Drop photos here or tap to chooseJPG, PNG or WebP. You can select many at once.

    Private by design: your files are processed on your device and are never sent to our servers.

    Some portals only accept very small pictures, and a signature or a small face photo has to fit under 20 KB. This tool lowers the picture quality step by step, and shrinks the pixel size only if it has to, until the file is 20 KB or less.

    It is the same idea as the Compress to KB tool inside the ScanOrbit app, so you get the same result on the web and on your phone.

    How to use

    1. Choose your photo, or drop it on the box.
    2. Leave the target at 20 KB, or type another number.
    3. Press Compress to KB, then Download.

    Tips

    • Start from the original photo, not a screenshot. Screenshots are already low quality.
    • If the result looks too soft, try 40 KB and see if the form still accepts it.
    • The result is saved as a JPG, because JPG gives the smallest file for photos. Transparent areas turn white.

    Questions

    How do I reduce an image to 20KB?

    Add your photo, keep the target at 20, and press Compress to KB. The tool shrinks it until it is 20 KB or smaller, then you download it.

    Does it upload my photo?

    No. The picture is processed inside your browser and never sent to a server.

    Will the 20KB photo look bad?

    It depends on the photo. Simple, bright pictures stay sharp. Very large or detailed photos lose some detail at 20 KB, so check the result before you upload.

    Can I do many photos at once?

    Yes. Add several photos and each one is compressed to the target size.
